01 / The short version

An unconventional
route to AI.

Different disciplines, same underlying curiosity: how do complicated systems work?

I have degrees in biology, psychology and mathematics, and have published research across all three. These days, I build AI systems.

My path has taken me through academic research, industry and international development. It hasn't been especially linear, but there is a common thread: understanding complex behaviour, then turning that understanding into something useful.

I'm particularly interested in the space where theoretical ideas become working software — and where an interdisciplinary perspective makes a practical difference.

Jack of all trades?Possibly. I prefer to think of it as an unwillingness to stop asking questions.
